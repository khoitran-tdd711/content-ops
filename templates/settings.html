OneUp connection

OneUp (oneupapp.io) is the service that actually posts to Instagram/LinkedIn/Facebook/TikTok on your behalf — this app hands scheduled posts off to it via API. Get your API key from oneupapp.io/api-access (you'll need a OneUp account with your social accounts already connected there). Leave the API key blank if you'd rather use the manual "send to OneUp" fallback for now.

Google Drive (for .zip files)

This lets the app open a Drive .zip and send every photo inside it straight to OneUp, instead of you having to paste individual photo links. It needs a Google service account key (a JSON file from Google Cloud) — create one, then paste its full contents below. After saving, click "Test connection" to see the service account's email address, then share your Drive folders with that exact email (same as sharing a folder with a coworker). Leave this blank if you'd rather keep pasting individual photo links — that still works.
